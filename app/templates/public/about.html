On-Road Cost & EMI Clarity

A property's listed price is never the final amount you pay. Every valuation automatically calculates your statutory Mumbai acquisition expenses:

  • Stamp Duty: 6% (5% standard + 1% Mumbai Metro Cess)
  • Registration Fee: Standard 1% government charge
  • Monthly EMI: 20-year home loan estimate at 8.5% interest (80% LTV)

Machine Learning Architecture

PropVal Insights uses an advanced Gradient Boosting regressor trained and cross-validated on 52,160 cleaned Mumbai property records across 224 Micro-Markets. During model benchmarking against Random Forest ensembles, Gradient Boosting achieved the highest predictive accuracy (Test R² = 0.8654) and lowest Mean Absolute Error, incorporating Target Encoding and logarithmic target regression.
